# Channel profile

Copy these into YouTube Studio under Customization. Files for the picture and banner are in this folder.

## Name

**Where the Money Goes**

It matches the line that closes every video ("if you want to keep seeing where the money really goes, subscribe"). I have not checked whether the name or handles are free on YouTube; check when you create the channel.

## Description (About section)

```
Every company has a business you can see and a business that actually makes the money. This channel is about the second one.

Why does Costco lose money on a $1.50 hot dog? Why are airline miles worth more than the planes? How did a $32 billion company disappear in nine days? Each video takes one question and follows the money until it makes sense.

What to expect:
• Hidden business models behind everyday companies
• Collapses and scandals, explained without the jargon
• The systems that shape your wallet: credit scores, subscriptions, loyalty points

Figures come from company filings, court records and published reporting. Illustrative examples are labelled on screen. Nothing here is financial advice.

New videos regularly. Subscribe to see where the money really goes.
```

The first two sentences are what shows in search results, so keep them first if you edit.

## Packaging

**Title options (A/B test in this order)**
1. The Dark Genius of Dollar Stores
2. Why Dollar Stores Aren't Actually Cheap

**Description (first two lines):**
There are more dollar stores in America than McDonald's and Walmart locations combined. Here is how a plain metal box in a small town became one of the most copied businesses in retail, and what it costs the people who shop there.

## Figures and where they stand

Checked by web search during production:

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Dollar General has more than 20,000 stores (20,893 in January 2026) | Confirmed |
| Dollar General net sales above $40 billion ($40.6 billion in fiscal 2024) | Confirmed |
| About 80% of its stores are in communities of 20,000 or fewer | Confirmed, the company's own figure |
| Dollar General net income fell 32% in fiscal 2024 | Confirmed |
| Dollar Tree moved from $1 to $1.25 in 2021; some items now up to $7 | Confirmed |
| Dollar Tree bought Family Dollar for about $9 billion in 2015 and agreed to sell it for about $1 billion in 2025 | Confirmed |
| At least 75 communities blocked a proposed dollar store since 2019 | Confirmed as a count by the Institute for Local Self-Reliance, an advocacy group |
| State settlements over shelf and register prices; Pennsylvania's "more than 40%" of inspections failed | Confirmed (Pennsylvania, New Jersey, Ohio, Vermont) |
| Journalists' comparisons finding higher unit prices | Confirmed (CBC Marketplace, The Hustle) |

## Packaging

**Thumbnail:** A plane on the left with a small grey "$11B" tag. A gold coin marked "MILES" on the right with a large yellow "$22B" tag. Three words of text at most.

**Description (first two lines):**
In 2020, United Airlines borrowed against its frequent flyer program, which was appraised at about twice the value of the whole airline. Here is how selling miles to banks became the real business of flying.
